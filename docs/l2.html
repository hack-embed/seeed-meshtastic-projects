05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.
Clear answers.

Something else on your mind?
Let's talk ↗

Where can I use my coupon?+

Get $15 off an order of $50 or more, including shipping, on Seeed Studio’s online store. The coupon applies to all Seeed Meshtastic and MeshCore products.

What’s included in the merchandise pack?+

A selection of Seeed goodies, such as pins, Velcro patches, and mugs. Contents vary depending on availability.

Where do I submit my social media links?+

Add them when you submit your project. If you share your project later, submit your social links separately through the same form and include a link to your original project.

Do I need to post on social media to earn a reward?+

No. A working project approved for the activity earns the Build Reward. Social sharing is optional and unlocks the Share Reward when your post reaches the required milestone.

Can I earn both rewards?+

Yes! Submit your working project for the Build Reward, then meet a social sharing milestone to earn the Share Reward too.

What should I include in my project submission?+

A short description, a cover image, setup instructions, and relevant links. Include photos or a video showing your project running on L2.

Does my project have to use Mesh networking?+

No. From pocket games and custom interfaces to hardware experiments, any working project built with L2 is welcome.

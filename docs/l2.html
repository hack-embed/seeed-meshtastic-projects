05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.
Clear answers.

Something else on your mind?
Let's talk ↗

Does my project need to use Mesh?+

No. Mesh and non-mesh projects are welcome. Your project needs to work on Wio Tracker L2 Pro.

What should I include in my submission?+

Add a project description, a cover image, the hardware used, and resource links. Show your working project with photos or a short demo. Setup instructions help other makers get started.

How do I receive the Build Reward?+

Submit your working L2 Pro project for review. Once approved, it will appear in the project hub and qualify for a $15 Seeed discount coupon on orders of $50 or more, including shipping.

Do I need to reach the milestone on every platform?+

No. Reach the listed milestone with a single post or video on any one supported platform, then submit the link for review. Your project must already be approved.

Can I submit a social link later?+

Yes. Use Submit Your Link in the Share Reward section. Include the GitHub issue URL of your approved project and your public post link. You do not need to submit your project again.

Will my email be public?+

No. Email is optional and recommended for reward follow-up. It is excluded from public GitHub submissions; the form provides a separate private email contact option.

Where can I ask about rewards or my submission?+

Contact sensecap@seeed.cc with your project or submission link. The team can help with review status, coupon redemption details, and merchandise arrangements.
